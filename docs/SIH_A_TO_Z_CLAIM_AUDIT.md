## Summary

14 claims identified as stronger than current implementation/evidence supports. None were fabricated maliciously — most are optimistic phrasing left over from earlier phases, and several (IMDAA/INSAT proxy labels, SHAP fallback badge) show the project already correcting itself in-place. The pattern across nearly all 14 items is the same: a true, narrower claim (VOBL-specific, proxy-labeled, heuristic) gets generalized upward in headline copy (README intro, page title, map legend) without the caveat traveling with it.
